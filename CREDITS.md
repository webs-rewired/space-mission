# Credits

## 3D models
- **Saturn V** by juli (https://sketchfab.com/juliwin), from https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/saturn-v-9256dc7668bf4d9aad937be48a83b96c, licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Split into its stages for the game (tools/split_saturn.py).
- **Spacecraft, Perseverance rover, Apollo Lunar Module, Mobile Launcher and other NASA models**: NASA 3D Resources (https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources), public domain. This includes Galileo, Pioneer 10, Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter, 2001 Mars Odyssey, NEAR Shoemaker, OSIRIS-REx, Rosetta and Stardust, shrunk for the game with gltf-transform (Galileo's body color was fixed: the source file had its black blankets as bright purple). The Perseverance wheels were split out of NASA's model so they can turn.
- **Falcon 9, Atlas V, SLS and Starship models**: downloaded by the team. Add each author and license from its download page here.
- **Rocks**: made for this game (tools: make_rocks.py), painted with the textures below.

## Textures and images
- **Lunar soil**: from NASA photo AS11-40-5878 (Buzz Aldrin's bootprint, Apollo 11), public domain.
- **Rock**: Rock023 from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rock023), CC0.
- **Planet and star textures**: Solar System Scope (https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), CC BY 4.0.
- **Moon and dwarf planet maps** (Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Enceladus, Phobos, Deimos, Pluto, Charon): NASA 3D Resources, "Images and Textures" (https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources), public domain. The black-and-white maps were tinted with each moon's real color, and Callisto's unphotographed patches were filled with terrain copied from the rest of its map (tools: make_moon_maps.py).
- **Titan**: drawn for this game to match what Cassini saw in natural color: orange haze with a darker northern hood (its surface is hidden).
- **Screen markers** (spacecraft marker, warning ring): made for this game.
- **Launch sky**: Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0.

## Sound and music
- **Mission audio**: NASA (Apollo 11 countdown and "The Eagle has landed", Apollo 13, Artemis I, Space Shuttle, Perseverance's microphones, InSight, Juno), public domain.
- **Sound effects**: Kenney (https://kenney.nl), Sci-Fi Sounds and Interface Sounds packs, CC0. The motor, latch, clank and crash sounds for cables, ramps, legs and the robotic arm are from the Sci-Fi Sounds pack.
- **Music**: "Space Music: Out There" by yd and "Outer Space Loop" by wipics, from OpenGameArt.org, CC0.
